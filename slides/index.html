the challenge

Type a place in the sky. Watch it change.

NASA asked for a public web tool that plays the SPHEREx images of any patch of sky like a short video, tested on five named objects, with WiseView as the model.

Find it

One map of the whole sky, in SPHEREx's 102 colours or older surveys, with a lens to pick a spot.

 02

Watch it move

Every exposure on one north-up grid, in any of the six detectors, with Gaia stars moved to each date.

Understand it

The story told three ways, for someone new, a student or a scientist, in English, Indonesian and Italian.
